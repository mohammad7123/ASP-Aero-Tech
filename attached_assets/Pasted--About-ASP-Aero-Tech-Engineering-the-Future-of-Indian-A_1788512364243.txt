# About ASP Aero Tech

## Engineering the Future of Indian Aerospace & Defence

**ASP Aero Tech** is an Indian technology and manufacturing company focused on the development of **unmanned aerial systems, drones, aerospace electronics, embedded systems, and mission-oriented technology solutions**.

Built on a strong software and technology foundation and strengthened by the experience of **senior defence professionals and IIT alumni**, ASP Aero Tech brings together software, electronics, aerospace engineering, and operational expertise to develop solutions designed for real-world requirements.

Our vision is simple:

**To build advanced, reliable, and indigenous technology for a stronger and more self-reliant India.**

# Our Approach

## Design. Engineer. Integrate. Deploy.

We believe successful aerospace technology comes from the integration of multiple disciplines.

**01 — Understand**

We begin by understanding the operational requirement, environment, constraints, and mission objectives.

**02 — Design**

Our multidisciplinary teams develop the architecture, electronics, software, and mechanical systems required for the solution.

**03 — Engineer**

We transform concepts into engineered prototypes through iterative development, testing, and refinement.

**04 — Integrate**

Hardware, software, communication, sensors, and mission systems are integrated into a unified platform.

**05 — Validate**

Systems are evaluated against defined performance, reliability, and operational requirements.

**06 — Deploy**

Our objective is to deliver practical, scalable technology capable of moving from the laboratory to real-world applications.
